More than a pattern.
A connection.

TATREEZ is a space dedicated to Palestinian embroidery — its beauty, its diversity and the people who keep this tradition present in everyday life.

Why this project exists

Keeping the thread visible.

This project brings together stories, embroidered details and regional garments to offer an accessible way to discover Palestinian Tatreez.

It is designed as an invitation to look closely: to notice the care in each stitch, explore the variety of textile traditions and appreciate the knowledge carried from one generation to another.

Rather than treating embroidery as something frozen in the past, TATREEZ presents it as a living practice — one that can be remembered, shared and reimagined.
