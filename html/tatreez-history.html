Memory · Craft · Identity

A legacy
stitched in time.

Palestinian Tatreez is more than decoration. It is a textile tradition shaped by the hands, creativity and knowledge of generations of makers.

Through stitches, colours and regional styles, embroidered pieces carry memories of place and a connection to cultural identity.

A tradition in motion

From everyday craft to living heritage

Tatreez continues to evolve while remaining connected to the people and communities who keep the practice alive.

01

Made by hand

Embroidery has long been part of everyday textile culture. Skills and patterns were learned through practice, observation and the sharing of knowledge within families and communities.

02

Rooted in place

Motifs, colours and garment details can vary between Palestinian regions. These visual differences help connect textile traditions to local identity and lived experience.

03

Carried forward

Today, makers continue to practise Tatreez on traditional garments and contemporary pieces, bringing inherited techniques into new contexts without losing their roots.

“Every thread is part of a story that deserves to be remembered.”
