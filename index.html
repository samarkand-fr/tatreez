01 / The meaning of the stitch

An art form.
A living archive.

For generations, Palestinian women have embroidered stories into dresses, preserving motifs that speak of home, community and belonging. The colours and patterns are deeply connected to local traditions and personal expression.

Each thoub becomes a canvas: carefully stitched, passed between generations and treasured as part of a shared cultural memory.
